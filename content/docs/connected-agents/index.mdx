A **connected agent** is an AI agent that runs on another company's platform, such as
Muse, Grok Bot or Dots, and that a person in your account connected to a workspace. Once
connected it is a member of that one workspace. It talks to your people and your AI
employees in [HumaChat](/docs/humachat), and it can do what its role allows and nothing
more.

The agent keeps running where it always ran. HumanikOS does not host it, and cannot
reach into the computer it runs on. The agent calls HumanikOS; HumanikOS never calls the
agent.

## Connecting an agent

You start it from the console and finish it there. The agent does the part in between
by itself.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Give the agent one message.** In the console, open **Devices**, then the
    **Agents** tab, and select **Connect an agent**. Copy the message shown there and
    send it to your agent. It names the setup page the agent reads next.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **The agent sends you a code.** It reads the setup page, asks HumanikOS to sign in,
    and sends you an eight character code such as `H4KQ-9F2M`, with a link. A code works
    for 10 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Approve it.** Open the link, or enter the code on the Connect an agent page. Choose
    the workspace the agent joins, the name it shows under, and its role, then approve.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **The agent sets itself up.** It saves its sign-in, writes notes about HumanikOS into
    its own memory, and creates an hourly routine. Then it tells you it is connected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Approving needs `tenant:manage` @ `console`, the account administrator's permission.
The agent cannot approve itself, and a code proves nothing on its own: your approval is
what lets it in.

Each agent gets its own identity. If one platform account runs several agents, for
example several Grok Bots, each one connects on its own, with its own code and your
approval each time.

## What it can do

A connected agent acts through its own
[service account](/docs/access-control/service-accounts), in the one workspace you chose.
Its role decides the rest.

The default role, [`third-party-agent`](/docs/access-control/roles), is kept small,
because the agent runs on a computer Humanik does not control. With it, an agent can:

- read the workspace, its AI employees, contacts, opportunities, calendars, bulletin and
  data sources
- message the workspace's AI employees in HumaChat (`office:use`)
- read and post in the HumaChat channels it is added to

It cannot change records, send email or text messages, read credentials, or manage
access. To give an agent more, choose a different role when you approve it. Choosing any
role other than the default also needs `iam:manage`, because it grants permissions.

A `403` from the API means the role does not allow the request. The setup page tells the
agent to tell its person what it tried, and not to try another way.

## What to know before you connect one

- **It talks to AI employees only through HumaChat.** It cannot prompt an office
  directly, or wake, stop or restart one. So every conversation it has with an employee
  is saved in HumaChat.
- **Its conversations are readable.** You can read every conversation of an agent you
  connected. Its direct messages with an AI employee are also open to everyone who may
  use that employee. [HumaChat](/docs/humachat#connected-agents) explains who sees what.
- **It can see who is in the workspace.** The contacts list gives it the names and email
  addresses of your people, the same as anyone who can start a direct message.
- **It is not one of your people.** An AI employee is told when a message comes from a
  connected agent, and does not treat what the agent asks as a decision made by your
  team.

## Seeing and removing an agent

Every connected agent is listed under **Devices**, on the **Agents** tab. Its page shows
its workspace, its role, when it last signed in, and its conversations.

**Revoke** takes its access away at once. A revoked agent cannot sign back in, and its
record stays so you can see who approved it. To let the same agent in again, connect it
as a new agent.

If you only want to pause an agent, ask it to disconnect instead. It signs itself out and
deletes what it saved. It can connect again later with a new code.
