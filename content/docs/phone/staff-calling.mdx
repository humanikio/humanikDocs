---
title: Calling from the browser
description: Your people dialing out from a business number, without a phone system on their desk.
---

A person can place a call from the workspace, in their browser, from a number the
workspace holds. The person they are calling sees the business number rather than
a personal one, and the call lands in the same conversation everything else does.

No handset, no desk phone, and nothing to install.

## What it takes

**The workspace needs a number that can make calls.** Staff calling does not
require the number to be assigned to an employee. A number allocated to the
workspace and nothing else is enough, which makes it a reasonable way to run a
line that only people use.

**The person needs `phone:call`.** This is a separate action from `phone:send`
for a specific reason: it is the ability to open a voice line as the business,
and read only roles should not have it.

<Callout>
  **`phone:call` is held by people, not by employees.** The built in
  [`officeAgent`](/docs/access-control/roles) role holds `phone:send` and not
  `phone:call`, so an employee cannot mint a browser calling credential. Employees
  place calls their own way, from their own machine, and that is covered in
  [Contacts and conversations](/docs/crm).
</Callout>

## Which number the call comes from

The person picks, and the pick is checked. A number that is not allocated to that
workspace is refused rather than substituted.

The check does not trust the browser. The credential minted for a session carries
both who the person is and which workspace they are acting in, it is signed, and
the from number is validated against that rather than against anything the page
sends. Someone editing values in their browser cannot call from another
workspace's number.

Credentials last an hour, so a long session renews rather than persisting.

## Outward only

<Callout type="warn">
  **You can call out from the browser. You cannot receive in it.** Inbound calls
  ring real phones, using the call forwarding number each person sets for
  themselves. See [Who answers](/docs/phone/answering).
</Callout>

## What is kept

A call placed this way is recorded only if `recordOutboundStaff` is on for that
number, and it is transcribed when it is recorded, with each side labelled. Both
are off by default. See
[Recording and transcripts](/docs/phone/recording-and-transcripts).

Either way the call appears in the contact's conversation, attributed to the
person who made it, with its direction, status and length. A person calling a
customer and an employee calling the same customer produce the same kind of
record in the same thread.
