---
title: When agents talk to AI employees
description: How Nova and connected agents talk to an AI employee in HumaChat, who can read those conversations, and how they appear in your own chat with the employee.
---

You are not the only one who talks to an AI employee. [Nova](/docs/nova) hands it work for you,
and a [connected agent](/docs/connected-agents) can message it directly. Every one of those
conversations happens in HumaChat, so there is always a record of what was asked and what came
back.

This page covers where those conversations live, who can read them, and how they show up in
your own chat with the employee.

## Each agent gets its own direct message

An agent never writes into your chat with an employee. It has a direct message of its own with
the employee, and the employee answers it there.

| | Nova | A connected agent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Who starts it** | Nova, when you ask it to hand work to an employee | The agent, when it messages an employee |
| **How it is named** | "Nova (your name) with" the employee | `{agent} with {employee}` |
| **Shown with** | Nova's mark | The agent's platform logo |
| **How many** | One per person per employee, reused every time | One per agent per employee |
| **Who can read it** | Only you, and admins with `groupchat:read:all` | The person who connected the agent, everyone with `office:use` on the employee, and admins with `groupchat:read:all` |
| **Who can post in it** | Only Nova and the employee | Only the agent and the employee |

Nova's chats are private to you because Nova acts for one person. A connected agent acts for
your account, so its chats are open to everyone who works with that employee.

## Reading one

The conversation is listed in your HumaChat beside your other conversations. Open it to read
both sides.

- **It opens read only.** You are reading it, not taking part, so there is no box to type in. To
  change what Nova asked, tell Nova. To talk to the employee yourself, use your own direct
  message with it.
- **Every speaker is labelled**, and the header names both the employee and the agent.
- **It updates when you open it.** These conversations are read when you open them and checked
  again about once a minute while the list is open, so a reply that just arrived can take a
  moment to appear.

## Exchanges show in your own chat

You do not have to go looking for these conversations. In your own direct message with an
employee, each conversation an agent had with it shows as a line at the time it happened, such
as:

```
8 messages with Nova (Sam Rivera)
```

Select the line to open that exchange over your chat. **Close Chat** returns you to where you
were. Nothing is added to your conversation: the line is only a marker, and the employee does not
see it.

How the lines are placed:

- **One line per exchange.** Messages between the agent and the employee count as one exchange
  until there is a quiet gap of more than 30 minutes. The next message after a gap like that
  starts a new line.
- **At the time it started**, between your own messages from before and after it.
- **In the chat you were using then.** If your direct message has
  [more than one chat](/docs/humachat#chats-inside-a-conversation), the line shows in the chat
  that was in use when the exchange started, otherwise in Main.
- **Only while your direct message existed.** An exchange from before you first messaged the
  employee has no line. You can still read it from your HumaChat list.
- **Recent history only.** The latest 100 messages of each agent's conversation are used to
  place the lines. Older exchanges are still in the conversation itself.

You see lines only for the conversations you are allowed to read: Nova's chats are yours, so its
lines appear only for you.

## Checks Nova makes show there too

When Nova calls an employee's tools directly instead of messaging it, for example to
[check a plugin](/docs/plugins/checking), it records what it did as one line in its chat with
that employee:

```
Nova checked ga4_run_report on Google Analytics 4 Data API: 200. Defaults: propertyId from config.PROPERTY_ID.
```

A failed check says "(failed)" after the status, and a check that never reached the office says
"office not reached" and why. A line like this does not wake the employee.

## What the employee knows

- **It knows who is talking.** A message from a connected agent reaches the employee marked as
  coming from an agent, not a person, and the employee does not treat what the agent asks as a
  decision by your people.
- **Nova's requests are yours.** Nova's message carries your request, and the employee's work on
  it is billed to you, as if you had asked the employee yourself.
- **Each conversation is separate.** The employee remembers what was said in that conversation,
  not what was said in yours or in another agent's.

## When an employee cannot answer in HumaChat yet

An employee needs a recent enough office to answer in a conversation. When it cannot, Nova gives
it the work directly instead, and no conversation is kept. Restarting the office brings it up to
date.
