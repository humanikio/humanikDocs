---
title: Synced channels
description: Keep a HumaChat conversation in step with a Telegram group, and where text message conversations with customers live.
---

A synced channel is another place the same conversation appears. A message sent in
HumaChat reaches it, and a message sent there appears in HumaChat.

Telegram is the channel you can connect. Text message conversations with customers
work differently, and they are covered at the end of this page.

## What connecting does

Connecting a conversation creates a private Telegram group for it, named after the
conversation, and adds the people and AI employees who are in the conversation at
that moment. From then on:

| Direction | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Telegram to HumaChat** | A message appears under the name of the person who wrote it. It always lands in the conversation's **Main** chat, because Telegram has no separate chats |
| **HumaChat to Telegram** | A person's message is posted in the group by a shared HumanikOS account, starting with **From:** and their name. An AI employee's reply is posted by that employee's own bot |

<Callout type="warn">
  **Every chat in the conversation is sent to Telegram, not only Main.** If a
  conversation has several chats, messages from all of them appear in the one
  Telegram group, including an employee's replies in them. Replies from Telegram
  come back into Main. Keep a task you do not want in the Telegram group in a
  conversation that is not synced.
</Callout>

From Telegram, photos and documents up to 50 MB come through. Stickers, voice notes
and videos do not.

## Before you connect

Three things have to be true, and they are set up in three different places.
Skipping one produces a quiet result rather than an error, so it is worth knowing
all three first.

**1. Each person links their own Telegram account.** In the workspace, open
**Settings**, then **Profile**, and link Telegram with your Telegram **username**.
The username is checked against Telegram when you link, so it has to exist. If you
do not have one, set it in Telegram first, under Settings then Username. Nobody can
do this for someone else, because it is their account.

**2. Each AI employee that should take part needs its own bot.** An employee speaks
in Telegram under its own name, so it needs its own Telegram bot rather than
borrowing anyone else's:

1. Open [@BotFather](https://t.me/BotFather) in Telegram.
2. Send `/newbot`, or pick a bot you already made.
3. Copy the HTTP API token it gives you.
4. In the workspace, open the office, then the employee, then **Gateways**, and
   paste the token into the Telegram card.

<Callout type="warn">
  **That token is a credential.** Anyone holding it can act as that bot. Paste it
  straight into the gateway card and do not forward it. If it is ever exposed,
  revoke it in @BotFather and add the new one.
</Callout>

**3. You need permission to manage the conversation.** Connecting, adding people
later and disconnecting all need `groupchat:manage`, which
[`workspaceAdmin`](/docs/access-control/roles) includes. You also have to be in the
conversation. **Sync** is only shown to people who hold that permission.

## Connecting

Open the conversation and use **Sync** in its header, then **Sync to Telegram**.
The dialog says what will happen. Choose **Connect Telegram**.

When it finishes, it tells you who was added. Anyone who could not be added is
named, so you know who still has a step to do, and there is an invite link you can
copy and share.

There is one other place to connect: starting a private chat with an employee from
its office on the Board. When both you and the employee are ready (your account is
linked and the employee has a bot), that dialog offers **Create the private
Telegram chat now**. The usual New message and New group flows do not connect
Telegram. Connect from the header afterwards.

The icons beside a conversation's name show where it is synced. Click them to see
everyone in the conversation and where each of them stands with the Telegram group:

| What the row says | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **In group** | They are in the Telegram group and their messages sync |
| **Invite sent** | Their privacy settings refused a direct add, so they were sent an invite link to accept |
| **+** | They are ready but not in the group yet. Shown only to people who can manage the conversation. Choose it to add them |
| **Set up bot** | An AI employee whose office has no Telegram bot yet. It opens the office's **Gateways** page |
| **Link Telegram** | You have not linked your own Telegram account yet. It opens your profile |
| **Has not linked Telegram** | Another person who has not linked their account. Only they can do that |

The information button in that list gives a short explanation.

## When a step is missing

Nothing here fails loudly, which is why the order matters.

| What was missing | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| A person has not linked Telegram | They are named after you connect, as somebody who could not be added |
| Their Telegram privacy settings refuse group invitations | They are sent an invite link instead, and have to accept it |
| An AI employee has no bot | It is not added. It still answers in HumaChat, and its replies are not posted in Telegram |
| Someone joined the Telegram group some other way | Their messages are not brought into HumaChat. Only people added through the sync are recognised |

## Changes after connecting

The sync keeps the membership it had when you connected. It does not follow later
changes on its own.

| Change | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Someone joins the conversation later** | They are not added to Telegram automatically. Anyone who can manage the conversation adds them with **+** in the list above. An administrator can also add them from the Console, under **HumaChat**, on the conversation's Telegram card |
| **Someone links Telegram after you connected** | The same: **+** appears next to their name once they have linked |
| **An AI employee gets a bot after you connected** | The same: **+** appears next to it once the bot is set up |
| **Someone leaves the conversation** | They stay in the Telegram group, and anything they write there is no longer brought into HumaChat. Remove them in Telegram if they should not see it |

## Replies

A reply made in Telegram shows in HumaChat as a reply, with the message it answers
quoted above it. Click the quote to go to that message.

Three limits apply for now:

- A reply to a message sent before replies were supported still shows the quote,
  but clicking it does not take you to the original.
- A reply made in HumaChat is not yet sent to Telegram as a reply. It arrives as an
  ordinary message.
- An AI employee reads the text of a reply, but is not told which message it
  answers.

## Disconnecting

Disconnect from the Console, under **HumaChat**, on the conversation's Telegram card,
with **Unlink**. Syncing stops in both directions.

The Telegram group itself is not deleted and nobody is removed from it, so people
there keep what was already said. The conversation's history in HumaChat is not
changed.

## Text message conversations with customers

A conversation with a customer by text message is not something you connect. It
exists because the customer texted one of your numbers, or because someone texted,
called or emailed that contact from the CRM.

These conversations are not in HumaChat's list. They are in the CRM, under
**Conversations**, next to the contact they belong to. There, a reply from an AI
employee or from someone on your team is sent to the customer as a text, from one of
your numbers.

STOP, START and HELP are handled before a message reaches the conversation, and they
count only when they are the whole message. See
[STOP, START and HELP](/docs/phone/texting#stop-start-and-help).
