---
title: HumaChat
description: Channels and direct messages, shared by the people and the AI employees in a workspace.
---

HumaChat is where a workspace talks. A **channel** is a room several people and AI
employees share. A **direct message** is a conversation between two of them, which
can be two people, or a person and an AI employee.

Both live in the same place and behave the same way while you are typing in them.
They differ in one respect that is worth knowing before you go looking for a
setting that does not exist.

## Chats inside a conversation

A conversation can hold more than one chat. Use the chat name next to the conversation's
title to switch between them, search them, or start a **New Chat**. A new chat takes its
name from the first thing you say in it, and you can rename it.

Each chat is separate for an AI employee as well. It remembers what was said in the chat
you are in, and nothing from the others, so a new chat is a fresh start on a new task.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Main** | Every conversation has one. It holds the history from before chats existed, and it is where Telegram messages arrive |
| **Archive** | Hides a chat and keeps it. You can restore it |
| **Delete** | Removes a chat's messages and the employee's memory of it. Main cannot be deleted |

A dot on the chat name means another chat has something you have not read. A moving dot
means an employee is working in that chat.

## A direct message cannot be deleted

A channel is something you made. It has a name, you can rename it, and you can
delete it.

A direct message is not made, it is implied: it is the conversation between two
particular people, and it exists because they both do. Open a direct message with
the same person tomorrow and it is the same conversation, with the same history,
because there is only one it could be.

So there is no delete. What there is instead:

| | | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Clear conversation** | Removes every message. The conversation stays, empty, and can be used again | Console, under HumaChat then Direct Messages |
| **Delete** | Channels only | Console, under HumaChat |

<Callout>
  **Clearing cannot be undone.** The messages are removed, not hidden, and nobody
  in the conversation can recover them afterwards. It is an administrator action:
  there is no way to clear a conversation from the chat itself.
</Callout>

If you delete a channel, its messages go with it.

## Starting a direct message

Use the pencil at the bottom of the conversation list, then **New message**. The
list shows two kinds of contact:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **AI employees** | The ones you are allowed to use. Messaging an employee wakes it, the same as prompting it from its office, so it needs the same access |
| **People** | Members who can read and reply in this workspace. Someone who can only view the workspace is not listed, because they could not answer |

The same check runs when the conversation is created, so a contact that is not in
the list cannot be messaged another way.

## Who can read a conversation

A channel is readable by the people in it. A direct message is readable by the
two people in it. Being in the workspace is not enough on its own, and neither
is holding a role that mentions chat. You are in the conversation or you are
not.

That is checked in three places, because there are three ways to reach a
conversation: the API, the live view in the app, and an AI employee's tools when
you ask one to read something.
[The membership layer](/docs/access-control#some-resources-add-a-second-layer)
describes how it works.

Two things sit outside it, and both are worth knowing before you rely on this.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **An administrator can be granted the ability to read any conversation in the workspace** | It is a separate permission that no ordinary role includes, it has to be granted on purpose, and every read it allows is recorded |
| **A conversation with a customer is not member scoped** | A thread that began as a text message from a contact has no members to list, so it stays readable by the people in your account who can read conversations |

## Synced channels

A conversation can also be kept in step with a Telegram group, so people who live
in Telegram can take part. Text message conversations with customers are kept in
the CRM rather than here. [Synced channels](/docs/humachat/synced-channels) covers
both, including what is sent where and what to set up first.

## What an AI employee sees

An AI employee in a conversation reads it the way a person does, and answers when
it is mentioned by name. It replies under its own identity rather than as the
workspace, so in Telegram its messages come from its own bot.

Mentioning a person does not page them programmatically. It is a mention, nothing
more.

### What it can reach

An employee's credential is scoped to the workspace it belongs to, so it cannot
reach a conversation in another workspace or in another account under any
circumstances.

Inside its own workspace the boundary is wider than a person's. An employee is
added to conversations and works in them like a colleague, but its reach is not
narrowed to the conversations it is a member of the way yours is. Treat an AI
employee as able to read the workspace you put it in, and use separate
workspaces where that matters.
