title: Nova and your AI employees

---

Your [Nova](/docs/nova) and your AI employees talk to each other in both directions. Both happen
in one place: a direct message in HumaChat between your Nova and the employee, listed as
"Nova (your name) with" the employee, and readable only by you.

| | Nova → employee | Employee → Nova |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **What it is for** | Work you want the employee to do | A change to the employee that only you can make |
| **Who starts it** | You, by asking Nova | The employee, while it works on something you asked for |
| **What happens** | The employee does the work and answers | Nova reads what it needs, and prepares each change as a card for you |
| **Whose permissions** | Yours | Yours, with stricter rules |
| **What needs your tap** | The same as when you ask Nova anything else | Every change |

## Nova hands work to an employee

This is the common direction. You ask Nova for something an employee does, for example "ask the
front desk to send today's bookings to Sam", and Nova sends it to the employee as a message. The
employee answers in the same chat, and Nova tells you what came back.

- **It needs `office:use` on the employee**, the same as messaging the employee yourself. Nova
  holds no access of its own.
- **The work is billed to you**, as if you had asked the employee directly.
- **Nova can show you what the employee did:** the answer, how long it took, and each tool it
  called with the arguments it used. This needs `office:read` on the employee.

[When agents talk to AI employees](/docs/humachat/agent-conversations) covers how these
conversations appear in HumaChat.

## An employee asks your Nova

HumanikOS keeps an employee's setup (its identity files, model, skills, protocols, plugins and
gateways) and gives the employee no tool to change it, so that an employee cannot quietly
rewrite its own rules. But an employee is often the first to notice that something needs to
change. When that
happens, it can send a message to your Nova, and your Nova prepares the change for you.

### When an employee uses it

| The employee finds that | It asks your Nova to |
| --- | --- |
| It keeps needing a rule or a fact it does not have | Add it to its `SOUL.md` or another identity file |
| It needs a different model or setting for the work | Change its agent config |
| It is missing a capability, or a skill is out of date | Add or change a skill, a protocol, or a plugin |
| You asked for a second employee for a separate job | Create a new office for that job |
| You asked for one of it per region or per client | [Duplicate](/docs/offices-and-employees/duplicating) its office |
| A gateway should be on or off, or its machine restarted | Change the gateway, or restart the machine |

An employee is told to use this only for changes it cannot make with its own tools. It manages
its own scheduled tasks itself, for example, so it does not ask Nova for those.

### When it can

- **Only while it works on something you started.** That means a message you sent it in
  HumaChat, a request on its page, or work your Nova handed it while you were using Nova. The
  message goes to the Nova of the person who started that work, and nobody else's.
- **Never from a scheduled task, a workflow, another agent or another employee.** There is no
  person behind those, so there is no Nova to ask. The employee is told it has nobody to ask.
- **Not while it runs two pieces of work at once.** It cannot tell which of the two you started,
  so it waits until only one is running.
- **Up to ten messages an hour** from one employee to your Nova.

### What Nova does with it

The words are the employee's, and an employee may be repeating something it read in an email,
a document or a web page. So Nova still acts as you, but under stricter rules than when you ask
it yourself:

- **It can read about your employees:** their setup, identity files, skills, protocols,
  schedules and plugins.
- **Every change becomes a card for you.** That covers identity files, settings, skills,
  protocols, schedules, gateways, plugins, waking or recreating a machine, creating an office
  and duplicating one. The card shows the exact change, and nothing happens until you tap
  **Approve**. A new office set up with its own model and `SOUL.md` is several cards.
- **Some things are refused outright:** billing, access control, credential values, your
  contacts, messages, databases, browsers, workflows, messaging other employees, and deleting
  an office or a plugin. Nova tells the employee that these need you, in the app.
- **Nothing the employee says counts as an answer.** A reply from the employee cannot approve or
  dismiss any card.
- **Your approval is checked when you tap.** If you no longer have the permission at that
  moment, nothing changes.

After you approve or the change fails, the employee gets a message in the same chat saying
which, so it can carry on with the work. [What Nova asks you to approve](/docs/nova/approvals#when-an-ai-employee-asks-your-nova)
lists the rules alongside the other kinds of approval.

### Why it works this way

Asking your Nova, rather than letting the employee change itself, keeps one rule true
everywhere: **a change to an employee is made by a person.** The employee can see what needs
changing, Nova can prepare it in full, and you decide. Because Nova runs as you, it can never
prepare a change you could not make yourself.
