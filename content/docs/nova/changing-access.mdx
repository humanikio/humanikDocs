---
title: Changing access with Nova
description: What Nova can change about who has access, what it will never change, and how you approve it.
---

In the console, you can ask Nova to change who has access to what. Nova does not
make the change itself. It prepares it, shows it to you on a card, and the change is
made only when you approve it.

<Callout>
  **Nova never grants admin-level access.** A role that can manage access control
  can grant any role to anyone, so only a person can give it out, on the IAM pages.
  This is checked by the platform, not left to the assistant. See
  [what Nova will never do](#what-nova-will-never-do).
</Callout>

## What Nova can prepare

- **Give a role** to one or more people, in one or more places. For example, the
  viewer role in one workspace.
- **Take a role away** from someone.
- **Give someone one role and remove everything else they hold.** For example,
  "view only in the Humanik workspace, and nothing else". The card lists every role
  that will be removed.
- **Create a custom role or policy**, when no existing role fits the request.

Nova looks for an existing role before it creates anything. When it does create a
policy, it describes the access in the same terms as the policy editor: each area,
such as Contacts or Billing, at view, use or manage. It does not write individual
permission names.

Everything for one request goes on one card. A request that would change nothing,
because the people already have exactly that access, gets no card. Nova tells you
instead.

## What Nova will never do

A role is **admin-level** when it can manage access control. Anyone who holds one
can grant any role to anyone, including themselves, so holding it is close to
owning the account. The built-in admin-level roles are Console Admin, IAM Manager
and Full Admin. A custom role that includes managing access control is admin-level
too. See [Roles](/docs/access-control/roles#admin-level-roles).

Nova will not:

- give anyone an admin-level role
- create a role or a policy that includes managing access control
- put managing access control into a new policy for any area

If you ask for any of these, Nova tells you it has to be done by a person, and
links to the IAM pages. A person who can manage access control can then assign it
there directly.

**Removing** an admin-level role is different. It lowers access, so Nova can
prepare it, and the card marks the role as admin-level.

<Callout type="warn">
  This limit is enforced by the platform. A proposal that contains admin-level
  access is refused before a card is created, and checked again at the moment you
  approve. Changing how you phrase the request does not change the result.
</Callout>

## Approving a change

The card lists each change in plain words, including every role that will be
removed. Nothing changes until you tap **Approve**. Replying "yes" in the chat does
not approve it: the card stays open until it is tapped or dismissed.

The card also warns you when a change:

- applies to the **whole account**, which includes workspaces created later
- would remove **your own** ability to manage access control. Once that is
  approved you cannot undo it yourself. The account owner or another admin would
  have to.

When you approve, the change is made **with your permissions**, and everything is
checked again at that moment. You must still hold `iam:manage` at `console`, and the
roles, policies and people on the card must still exist. If anything is no longer
valid, nothing is changed and Nova tells you why.

The parts are applied in this order: a new policy, then a new role, then the roles
being given, then the roles being removed. Giving before removing means a person
moved to a narrower role is never left with no access in between. If one part
fails, Nova reports exactly which parts were done.

## Who can ask

The person asking needs two things: permission to use Nova, and `iam:manage` at
`console`. How roles, policies and assignments fit together is explained in
[Access control](/docs/access-control).

## From a text message or a scheduled task

Nova can prepare an access change when you [text it](/docs/nova/texting) or from a
[scheduled task](/docs/nova/scheduled-tasks). The card waits in the app, and it is
approved the same way: by tapping it there.

## When a change takes effect

The platform reads a person's roles on every request, so an approved change applies
from their next request. A Nova task that is already running keeps the permissions
it started with.

## Not available from chat yet

Editing or deleting an existing role or policy. Use the IAM pages in the console for
those.
