---
title: What Nova asks you to approve
description: Which of Nova's actions wait for your approval, how you give it in the app, by text and from a scheduled task, and what Nova does without asking.
---

Nova acts as you. For most requests it does the work as soon as you ask: it sends
the message, updates the record, runs the workflow. A small number of actions are
different. Nova prepares them and then waits for you to approve.

This page lists those actions and explains how approval works in each place you can
reach Nova.

## Most work does not wait

When you ask Nova to do something ordinary, such as send a message, edit a contact
or wake an office, it does it with your permissions and tells you what it did. There
is no separate approval step.

Nova is instructed to check with you before a step that is hard to undo, such as a
delete, and it usually does. That is an instruction Nova follows, not a lock. If
you want an action to be impossible for someone, remove the permission from their
role. See [Access control](/docs/access-control).

## What waits for your approval

For the actions below, Nova cannot do the work itself. It shows you a card, and the
action happens only when the card is approved.

| Action | In the app | By text message | From a scheduled task |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Create a scheduled task** | A card. Tap **Approve** | Created straight away (see below) | A card waits in the app |
| **Change access** (roles, policies, who holds them) | A card. Tap **Approve** | A card waits in the app | A card waits in the app |
| **Save credentials** for an integration | A secure form in the app | Not possible by text | The form waits in the app |
| **Add your own model provider key** | A secure form in the app | Not possible by text | The form waits in the app |

Credentials and keys are never typed into a chat or a text message. The form writes
them straight to secure storage, and Nova does not see them.

## Approving in the app

The card shows exactly what will happen. For a scheduled task that is the full
instruction and the schedule. For an access change it is every role given and every
role removed.

**Tap Approve or Dismiss on the card itself.** For a scheduled task or an access
change, replying "yes" in the chat does not approve it. The card stays open, and
Nova tells you it is still waiting.

When you approve, the action is done as you, with your permissions at that moment.
An access change is checked again in full at that point: if you no longer hold the
permission, nothing changes and Nova tells you why. See
[Changing access](/docs/nova/changing-access).

If the action fails and nothing was changed, the card stays open so you can try
again. If part of it was done, the card closes so nothing is done twice, and Nova
tells you which parts happened.

Some cards are general questions, such as confirming a plan or choosing between
options. You can answer those by tapping, or by replying in your own words.

## Approving by text message

A text message cannot show a card. When you [text Nova](/docs/nova/texting):

- **A scheduled task you ask for is created straight away.** Your text is the
  request, so there is nothing further to approve. This applies to one time and
  repeating tasks alike.
- **Anything else that needs approval waits in the app.** Nova tells you so, and the
  end of its reply names what is waiting.
- **Credentials and keys cannot be added by text.** Nova tells you to use the app.

When a scheduled task is created from a text, the reply always ends with a line
naming it and how to remove it, for example:

```
Done: Schedule: Weekly report. Text "cancel Weekly report" to remove it.
```

<Callout type="warn">
  **If that line arrives for a task you did not ask for, cancel it.** Anyone who
  knows your number can send a message that looks like it came from you. The reply
  still goes to your real phone, which is why it always names what was created.
  Then check your list of scheduled tasks in your Nova settings.
</Callout>

## Approvals from a scheduled task

A [scheduled task](/docs/nova/scheduled-tasks) runs when nobody is watching. If it
prepares something that needs approval, the card waits in the app until you tap it.
Nothing is approved on your behalf.

A scheduled task cannot approve its own cards, and it cannot create another
scheduled task without you. If a task that runs often asks for the same kind of
approval again, the new card replaces its older open one, so only the latest is waiting.
