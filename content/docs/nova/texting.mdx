---
title: Texting Nova
description: Assign a phone number to Nova, and reach your own assistant from your handset.
---

A phone number in your organization can be given to Nova instead of to an office.
Text that number from your phone, and your own Nova answers: your model, your
permissions, your budget. The conversation also appears in your normal Nova chat,
so you can pick it up in the app afterwards.

This is the assistant, not a receptionist. If you want a number that answers your
customers, allocate it to an office instead. See
[Phone](/docs/phone).

## Assigning a number

In the console, go to **Assets → Phone Numbers**, open a number, and use the
**Assignment** tab. The Nova section there shows the workspace that owns the
number and a button to switch it.

<Callout type="warn">
  **A number can be given to Nova or an office, not both.** This is the same rule
  that lets a number receive inbound for only one office. Switching removes the
  previous assignment, and the console tells you exactly what it is about to
  remove before you confirm.
</Callout>

The number must be able to receive SMS. A voice only number cannot be assigned.

The same switch is available over the API, under
[Phone numbers and email](/docs/api-reference/phone-and-email).

Once assigned, the number appears under **Assets** in that workspace's Nova
settings, where members can see it without console access.

## Every person has to add their own number first

<Callout type="warn">
  **Assigning the number is only half of it.** Nova works out who is texting by
  matching the sender against the mobile number on that person's own profile.
  Anyone who has not added and confirmed theirs gets **no reply at all**, and
  nothing explains why. This is the step people miss.
</Callout>

Each member does this themselves, in their own workspace:

<Steps>

<Step>

### Open Settings then Profile

In your workspace, go to **Settings → Profile**. The first card is your mobile
number.

</Step>

<Step>

### Add your number and save

Enter it and press **Save**. A number can be on **one account only** across your
organization: if a colleague already has it, the save is refused and names them.

</Step>

<Step>

### Confirm it with a code

Saving is not the same as confirming. Press **Confirm with a code**, and we text
a six digit code to that handset. Enter it and the number is confirmed.

Saving a number always clears its confirmation, so a number you change has to be
confirmed again. Call forwarding keeps working either way; only texting Nova
needs the confirmation.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Who gets an answer

Four things must all be true:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **You are in the organization that owns the number** | People outside it are not matched |
| **Your profile number matches the one you texted from** | Settings → Profile |
| **That number is confirmed** | The code step above |
| **You can use Nova in that workspace** | The same permission as opening Nova in the app |

If any of them fails, nothing runs and you get no reply. Silence is deliberate:
answering an unrecognised number would mean answering as somebody.

### Two people cannot share a number

Saving a number a colleague already has is refused, and the message names them.
On older accounts that were set up before this rule, a shared number means texts
from it are refused rather than answered as one of the two. Remove it from one
profile.

## What a reply looks like

Nova answers with a fresh text message, not as a reply attached to yours. It
often takes longer than an ordinary text: a request that needs Nova to go and do
something can run for a minute or more, and the answer arrives when the work
finishes.

Answers are written for a phone, so they are shorter than the same answer in the
app. Ask in the app when you want the long version.

## Sending a photo

You can attach a photo to your message. It is saved in your Nova chat in the app,
so it is there after the conversation and you can refer back to it later.

A photo on its own is a normal message. Nova will ask what you want done with it.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Works | Photos |
| Per message | One file. If you attach several, the first is the one Nova reads |
| Size | 5MB, which is the limit carriers place on picture messages |

Sending a photo costs the same as sending a message.

<Callout type="warn">
  **Documents cannot be sent by text.** A PDF, a spreadsheet or a Word file will
  not arrive, and usually shows as **Not Delivered** on your phone. This is not a
  setting you can change: mobile carriers decide what a picture message may
  carry, and outside of photos they carry very little. Upload the file in the app
  instead, where Nova can read it.
</Callout>

If a document ever does get through, Nova reads PDFs and plain text files. Treat
that as luck rather than something to rely on.

## Costs and limits

A Nova run started by text spends exactly as one started in the app: against your
budget, in your workspace, on whichever model you have chosen.

If your organization is out of credit, or your budget for the period is spent,
you get a text saying so rather than silence.

## Opting out

**STOP, START and HELP work on a Nova number, exactly as they do on any other
number of ours**, and they are handled before anything else. Texting STOP stops
Nova texting you. This is required of every number that sends text messages and
it cannot be switched off.

## The sharp edges

**Anyone who knows your number can send a message that looks like it came from
you.** Caller ID is not proof of identity, on any platform. Two things limit what
that is worth: the reply goes to the real handset rather than to whoever sent the
message, so no information comes back to them, and every exchange appears in your
normal Nova chat, where you will see work you did not ask for. Treat an
unexpected conversation in your history as worth investigating.

**The number does not answer calls.** Assigning it to Nova covers text messages.
A call to the same number is not answered by Nova.

**Nova cannot text a file back.** It can read what you send. It cannot send one.
When the result of a request is a file, Nova says what it made and you open it in
the app.

**Your profile number is also used for call forwarding.** It is one field, so
changing it changes both. Forwarding works whether or not the number is
confirmed; texting Nova does not.
