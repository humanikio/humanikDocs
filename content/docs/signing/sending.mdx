Select **Send** in the document editor. The send dialog shows the people again, so you can
make a last change, and asks how they should get their link.

## How people get their link

| Choice | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Email** | Each person gets an email with their own link. This is the default |
| **Text message** | Each person gets a text message with their own link |
| **Email and text message** | Both. One that arrives is enough |
| **Links only** | Nothing is sent. You hand each person their link yourself |

**Email** comes from the workspace's own email address when it has one, and from the
HumanikOS address when it does not.

**Text messages** come from a phone number in the workspace that can send text messages.
Every person needs a mobile number. The same checks apply as for any text message from the
workspace: a contact who has asked not to be contacted, or a number that has replied STOP, is
not sent to.

The send dialog shows, for each person, whether the chosen way can reach them, and says why
not when it cannot. If a message cannot be sent, that person stays unsent and you can try
again from the document page.

## Each person's link

A signing link is a secret. Anyone who has it can sign as that person.

- After sending, the dialog shows each person's link, with **Copy**.
- On the document page, each person's row shows their current link, with **Copy**. Looking
  at it does not change it, so the link they were sent keeps working.
- **New link** makes a fresh link for one person and nothing is sent. The link they had
  before stops working.
- **Resend** sends a fresh link the same way the document was sent (with **Links only**,
  nothing is sent). The link they had before stops working.

A link stops working when the person finishes or declines, when the document is cancelled or
expires, and when a newer link is made for that person.

In a signing order, people who sign later have no link until it is their turn.

## Sign-by date

You can set a date after which the document can no longer be signed. It must be more than
an hour ahead when you send, and at most a year ahead. When it passes, the document
**expires** and every link stops working.

## Reminders

People who have not signed yet are reminded every 2, 3 or 7 days, or not at all. The
default is every 3 days. Each person gets at most 3 reminders, none after the sign-by date,
and none before it is their turn. With **Links only**, no reminders are sent.

<Callout type="info">
  **Each reminder has a new link, and the old link stops working.** A person who opens an
  older message sees that the link no longer works. They should use the newest one.
</Callout>

## Signers from the CRM

A person can be a [contact](/docs/crm) instead of a typed name. Their name, email address
and mobile number come from the contact, and are read again when a draft is sent, so a
change in the CRM is used. A contact who is archived, has asked not to be contacted, or has
no usable address is refused.

Choosing a contact needs `contact:read` in the workspace, as well as `signing:send`.
