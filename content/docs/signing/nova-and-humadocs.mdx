[Nova](/docs/nova) can work in HumaDocs for you. It can place and review the fields on a
template or a draft document, and send a template to people for signing. Nova never changes
anything on its own: every change and every send is shown to you on a card first, and happens
only when you select **Approve**.

## Auto place and review document fields

In the template editor or the document editor, select **Auto place and review document
fields** at the top of the left panel. Nova opens on the right with a new chat about that
template or document. Then Nova:

1. Reads the roles and the fields already placed.
2. Finds the blanks on the pages: typed lines (`____`), drawn lines to write on, empty answer
   boxes and checkboxes, together with the words printed around each one.
3. Decides each field from those words: a signature, initials, the date signed, a name, an
   email address, a checkbox or a text answer, which role it belongs to, and whether it is
   required.
4. Checks the result for problems, such as two fields on top of each other or a role with no
   signature.
5. Shows you one card with every change, grouped by page, and anything it was not sure of.

When you approve, the fields are saved and appear in the editor within a few seconds. While
Nova is open, the editor folds its right panel so you can watch the pages.

If the document already has fields, Nova reviews them in the same pass and suggests fixes,
such as a field given to the wrong role. It does not move fields that are fine.

You can also ask Nova in your own words from any HumaDocs page, for example "add a signature
and date for the tenant on the last page". Nova knows which template or document you have
open.

## What Nova places, and what it does not

- Nova places fields next to words that are printed on the page or on blanks it found. It does
  not guess positions.
- Nova never changes the text of the PDF. To change the wording of a document, edit the PDF
  first, then upload it as a new template.
- A scanned PDF has no text Nova can read. Place its fields by hand.
- A document that has been sent cannot change. Nova can change its template or a copy instead.

## Who can approve

The card can be approved only by someone who could make the same change in the editor:

| Change | Needs |
| --- | --- |
| Fields on a template | Permission to manage signing templates |
| Fields on a draft document | Permission to send signing documents |
| Sending a template | Permission to send signing documents |

If you or a teammate save the fields after Nova read them, the card is refused when you
approve it, and Nova reads the fields again. Nobody's changes are overwritten without warning.

## AI employees

When one of your AI employees asks Nova for help with a document, Nova can read the fields
and find the blanks, and any change it proposes still waits on a card for you. AI employees
cannot send documents for signing through Nova.

## Sending

Nova can list your templates and send one, with one person for each role. The card names
every person and how they get their link, and nothing is sent until you approve. Nova never
shows signing links. See [Sending](/docs/signing/sending).
