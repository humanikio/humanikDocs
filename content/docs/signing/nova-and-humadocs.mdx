[Nova](/docs/nova) can work in HumaDocs for you, from start to finish. It can make a template or
a document from a PDF, copy one, put a new PDF behind its fields, place and review the fields,
name the people who sign, and send it. Anything that changes what you already have, or contacts
anyone, is shown to you on a card first and happens only when you select **Approve**.

## A whole job

You can ask for everything at once, for example: "Here's our lease. Send it to Sam Rivera from
the CRM as the tenant, and to Jo Park, jo@example.com, 555 0100, as the guarantor, by text."
Nova then:

1. Takes the PDF you attached.
2. Makes a draft document from it. A PDF you send once does not become a template, so your
   templates list stays tidy. Ask for a template if you will use it again.
3. Places the fields for each person and shows you **the first card: the fields**.
4. Finds Sam Rivera in your CRM. If two contacts match, Nova asks which one.
5. Adds both people to the draft, and tells you if anything is still missing.
6. Shows you **the second card: the send**, with every person, their email and phone, the order
   they sign in, and who it is sent from. The messages go out when you approve.

## Where the PDF can come from

- **Attached to your chat with Nova.** Nova can use a PDF from any earlier message in the same
  chat, not only the last one. Only you can use the files in your own chats.
- **A bucket in your workspace.** Files your AI employees save, such as a PDF they edited, are
  found by name or folder. Nova needs your permission to read that bucket.

Files in Google Drive, email attachments and files posted in HumaChat cannot be used yet:
attach the PDF to your chat with Nova, or save it in a bucket. HumaDocs takes PDFs up to 25 MB
and 200 pages, with no password.

## Auto place and review document fields

In the template editor or the document editor, select **Auto place and review document
fields** at the top of the left panel. Nova opens on the right with a new chat about that
template or document. Then Nova:

1. Reads the roles and the fields already placed.
2. Finds the blanks on the pages: typed lines (`____`), drawn lines to write on, empty answer
   boxes and checkboxes, together with the words printed around each one.
3. Decides each field from those words: a signature, initials, the date signed, a name, an
   email address, a checkbox or a text answer, which role it belongs to, and whether it is
   required.
4. Checks the result for problems, such as two fields on top of each other or a role with no
   signature.
5. Shows you one card with every change, grouped by page, and anything it was not sure of.

When you approve, the fields are saved and appear in the editor within a few seconds. While
Nova is open, the editor folds its right panel so you can watch the pages.

If the document already has fields, Nova reviews them in the same pass and suggests fixes,
such as a field given to the wrong role. It does not move fields that are fine.

You can also ask Nova in your own words from any HumaDocs page, for example "add a signature
and date for the tenant on the last page". Nova knows which template or document you have
open.

## What Nova places, and what it does not

- Nova places fields next to words that are printed on the page or on blanks it found. It does
  not guess positions.
- Nova never changes the text of the PDF. To change the wording, the PDF is edited first (by you,
  or by an AI employee with PDF tools that saves it in a bucket). Nova then puts the new PDF
  behind the template's fields with **Replace PDF**, and the fields stay where they were.
- A scanned PDF has no text Nova can read. Place its fields by hand.
- A document that has been sent cannot change. Nova can make a copy as a new draft, change it,
  and send that.

## What waits for a card

| Nova does | Card first? | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lists and reads templates and documents | No | Permission to view signing |
| Makes a template, or copies one | No | Permission to manage signing templates |
| Makes a draft document, or copies a document | No | Permission to send signing documents |
| Names the people on a draft | No | Permission to send signing documents, and to view CRM contacts for a contact |
| Puts a new PDF behind a template's fields | Yes | Permission to manage signing templates |
| Puts a new PDF behind a draft's fields | Yes | Permission to send signing documents |
| Changes fields on a template | Yes | Permission to manage signing templates |
| Changes fields on a draft | Yes | Permission to send signing documents |
| Sends a document or a template | Yes | Permission to send signing documents |

New templates and drafts are private to your workspace, and nobody is contacted until you approve
the send card, which lists everyone again. A card can be approved only by someone who has the
permission in the last column. A PDF from a bucket also needs permission to read that bucket.

When Nova puts a new PDF behind the fields, the card shows the page count before and after. If
the new PDF has fewer pages and fields sit on the missing pages, Nova asks you whether to delete
those fields or move them to the last page.

Nova only shows you a card that can work. If something would stop it, for example the fields
were saved by you or a teammate after Nova read them, the new PDF is the same file, or a person
is missing for a send, Nova is told why straight away, fixes it or asks you, and then shows the
card. If the fields change between the card and your approval, the change is refused and the card
closes, and Nova reads the fields again. Nobody's changes are overwritten without warning.

## AI employees

When one of your AI employees asks Nova for help with a document, Nova can read templates,
documents and fields and find the blanks. Anything it would make or change waits on a card for
you, including a new template or draft. AI employees cannot send documents for signing through
Nova.

## The people who sign

Each role gets one person, in one of two ways:

- **A contact in your CRM.** The name, email and phone come from the contact record.
- **Details you give Nova**, or that are part of the job: a name and email, and a mobile number
  if they should get a text.

Nova never guesses an email address or a phone number. If something is missing, it asks.

## Sending

Nova sends by email, text message, both, or links only. The send card names every person and
how they get their link, and nothing is sent until you approve. Nova never shows signing links:
with links only, copy each one from the document page. See [Sending](/docs/signing/sending).
