A signer opens their link in any browser. They do not need an account and they do not
install anything.

## 1. Read, then agree

The page shows who sent the document, its name, any message from the sender, and the
sign-by date. The document itself is shown below, so the signer can read it before
anything else.

Before they can fill anything in, the signer agrees to sign electronically. The page says
they can ask the sender for a paper copy instead. The time they agreed, and the exact words
they were shown, are recorded and printed on the audit pages.

## 2. Fill in, with a guide

After they agree, a bar at the top shows how many required fields are done, for example
**2 of 5 required fields done**, and one button that leads the way:

| The button says | It does |
| --- | --- |
| **Start** | Goes to the first required field |
| **Next** | Goes to the next required field that is still empty |
| **Finish** | Shown when every required field is filled. Finishes signing |

The guide visits required fields only, in reading order. Optional fields are filled by
selecting them on the page, or from the list of fields beside the document. Each jump
scrolls the field to the middle of the screen, and a text field is ready to type in.
Pressing Enter in a text field moves to the next one.

On a computer, an arrow beside the document points at the current field and does the same as
the button. On a phone, the button sits in a bar at the bottom of the screen, where the thumb
is.

How fields look:

| Field | Looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Required, still empty | Shaded, in the HumanikOS purple |
| Optional, still empty | A dashed outline |
| The one being filled | A soft pulsing ring, with its name above it |
| Filled | A green check |
| Filled in by the sender | Shown with its value. It cannot be changed unless the sender allowed it |
| Date signed | Grey, with a lock. It shows today's date as soon as the signer signs, and it cannot be changed |

Signers see only their own fields. They see the names of other people signing and whether
each has signed, never their email addresses. Answers are saved as they go, so a signer can
close the page and come back to the same link later.

## 3. Sign

Selecting a signature field opens a pad with three ways to sign:

- **Draw**, with a mouse, a trackpad or a finger
- **Type** the name, drawn in a handwriting style
- **Upload** a picture of a signature

The first signature is adopted. Every other signature field then signs with one select, and
the signer can still change any of them. Initials work the same way, separately from the
signature. Each field is something the signer chose to sign: nothing is filled in on their
behalf.

As soon as the first signature or initials is in place, every date signed field shows today's
date, in the signer's own time zone. The signer cannot change it. It is the same date that
appears in the finished document.

## 4. Finish

**Finish** can always be selected. If a required field is still empty, every empty one is
marked in red, the page scrolls to the first of them, and a message says how many are left.

When nothing is missing, HumanikOS checks every required field again on the server, fills in
the date signed in the signer's own time zone, and records the time, the address and the
browser they signed from. The link then stops working.

When the last person finishes, everyone gets an email with a link to download the finished
document. That link works for 30 days. The document is never sent as an attachment.

## Declining

A signer can **Decline to sign**, with a reason if they want to give one. Declining stops
the whole document: nobody else can sign it, every link stops working, and the sender is
told by email with the reason.

## When a link does not open

| The page says | Because |
| --- | --- |
| Not your turn yet | In a signing order, someone signs first. The page names who. The link works, and the signer can sign with it once those people have signed |
| This link no longer works | A newer link was made for them with **Resend** or **New link**. They should use the newest message |
| You have already signed | They finished. The finished document arrives by email when everyone has signed |
| This request was cancelled, has expired, or was stopped | The sender cancelled it, the sign-by date passed, or someone declined |
