HumaDocs turns a PDF into something people can sign online. You prepare the PDF once as a
**template**, then send it as a **document** to the people who need to sign. Each person gets
their own private link. They do not need an account.

When the last person has signed, HumanikOS makes the finished document: every answer and
signature drawn onto the PDF, audit pages added at the end, and a digital seal that shows if
anyone changes the file later. Everyone receives a copy.

## Who can do what

Three actions control HumaDocs in a workspace:

| Action | Lets someone |
| --- | --- |
| `signing:read` | See templates and documents, who has signed, the activity, and download finished documents |
| `signing:send` | Send a template, change and send drafts, send a new link, see each person's link, and cancel a document |
| `signing:manage` | Create, change and archive templates, and delete documents |

The built-in [`workspaceAdmin`](/docs/access-control/roles) role holds all three.
`officeOperator` holds `signing:read` and `signing:send`. `viewer` holds `signing:read`.
Choosing a CRM contact as a signer also needs `contact:read`.

Signers need none of these. Their link is their access. See
[Access control](/docs/access-control) for how actions are granted, and
[Actions](/docs/access-control/actions) for every endpoint each one allows.

## With other parts of HumanikOS

- **CRM.** A signer can be a [contact](/docs/crm). Their name, email address and mobile
  number come from the contact, and a contact who has asked not to be contacted is refused.
- **Nova.** [Nova](/docs/nova) can list your templates and send one for you. The send is
  held until you approve it in the chat, the same way as Nova's other
  [approvals](/docs/nova/approvals). Nova never shows signing links.
- **API.** Everything on these pages can be done with a
  [service account](/docs/access-control/service-accounts) key. See the
  [API reference](/docs/api-reference/signing).

## Limits

- A PDF can be up to 25 MB and 200 pages. A password protected PDF cannot be used.
- A template has 1 to 10 roles and up to 500 fields.
- HumaDocs places fields on a PDF. It does not edit the PDF's own text. To change the
  wording, change the PDF and upload it as a new template.
- Chinese, Japanese and Korean characters are not drawn onto the finished document yet.
  They appear as question marks, and the audit pages say so. The answers themselves are
  kept as typed.
