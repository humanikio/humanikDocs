---
title: Workspaces
description: What a workspace is, what lives in it, and how to copy or remove one.
---

A workspace groups AI employees, data, automations and conversations, so separate teams or
separate clients can work in one account without seeing each other's work. Almost every other
call in this API takes a `workspaceId`, and almost every permission names a workspace, which
makes it the practical unit of access: scope someone to one workspace and they see only that
workspace's work.

A workspace itself is small: a name and a timezone. The things you think of as being in it (its
offices, databases, workflows, contacts, phone numbers) each record which workspace they belong
to.

## What a new workspace starts with

A workspace you create starts with a [namespace](/docs/namespaces) called `knowledgeBase`, with a
`documents` bucket and a `/knowledgeBase/company/` folder. It is the place for documents about your company and how
you work. Nova checks it at the start of each new chat, and your AI employees can read it too.
If you signed up through the AI Growth Plan, `/knowledgeBase/company/company.md` already
describes your business and your plan. Edit it or add files any time.

A copy of a workspace does not get a new, empty one. It gets the original's, with its files when
you choose to copy files. See [Duplicating a workspace](/docs/workspaces/duplicating).

## Working with workspaces

- **Create, rename and list** them in the console, or with
  [Create a workspace](/docs/api-reference/workspaces/workspaces/createWorkspace) and
  [List workspaces](/docs/api-reference/workspaces/workspaces/listWorkspaces).
- **Copy one** to start a second workspace set up the same way, for a new location or a new
  client. See [Duplicating a workspace](/docs/workspaces/duplicating).
- **Delete one** with everything it holds. Phone numbers, email addresses and Telegram groups are
  released rather than deleted, so you can give them to another workspace. See
  [Delete a workspace](/docs/api-reference/workspaces/workspaces/deleteWorkspace).
