---
title: Duplicating an office
description: Copy an AI employee's setup into a new office in the same workspace, what the copy gets and does not get, and how to follow it as it is made.
---

Duplicating an office makes a second AI employee that starts where the first one is: the same
identity files, skills, memory, plugins and scheduled tasks. The copy is a new office with its
own machine, its own API key and its own identity. From the moment it is made, the two are
separate, and a change to one does not reach the other.

## When to duplicate

- **One employee per region, team or client.** The front desk for one location, copied for the
  next, then given its own phone number.
- **A new job that starts from a working setup.** Copy the employee that already has the right
  plugins and rules, then change its `SOUL.md` for the new job.
- **Trying a change safely.** Copy the employee, change the copy's model or rules, and compare,
  while the original keeps working.

If you want a new employee with nothing in it, [create an office](/docs/api-reference/offices/offices/createOffice)
instead.

## Three ways to start one

- **Ask your [Nova](/docs/nova)**, for example "duplicate the front desk as Front desk (West)".
  Nova asks you to confirm first.
- **An AI employee can ask your Nova for a copy of itself** while it works on something you
  asked it to do. That always waits as a card for you to approve. See
  [Nova and your AI employees](/docs/nova/employees).
- **Call the API** with [Duplicate an office](/docs/api-reference/offices/offices/duplicateOffice).

Whichever you use, you need `office:manage` on the office you copy and on the workspace, the
same permission it takes to change the original and to create an office.

## What the copy gets

| The copy gets | The copy does not get |
| --- | --- |
| Its identity files, such as `SOUL.md` | Its HumaChat conversations. People start a new chat with the copy |
| Its model and settings, skills and look | Telegram and WhatsApp |
| Its memory, unless you ask for it without | Phone numbers and email addresses |
| Its own past sessions, as context | Channels it is a member of |
| Its plugins and protocols, with their credentials | Connected accounts, calendars and browsers |
| The databases, apps and websites it is connected to | Workflows that name the original |
| Its repeating scheduled tasks, paused | One-time scheduled tasks |
| Its machine size and idle timeout | Files it created itself on its machine |

- **Scheduled tasks arrive paused**, so a task that sends an email or posts to a channel does not
  run twice. Turn them on when the copy is ready, or ask for them to arrive as they are on the
  original.
- **Credentials are copied, not shared.** The copy has its own copy of each credential that
  belonged to the original alone, so changing one office's credentials does not change the
  other's. Credentials shared across your account stay shared.
- **A database is connected only if you can read it.** If you cannot, the copy is made without
  it, and the copy's report says so.
- **Its past sessions are context, not conversations.** The copy remembers what it worked on,
  but nobody sees a chat with it until they start one.
- **Workflows are not changed.** A workflow that sends work to the original still sends it to
  the original. Edit the workflow if the copy should get some of that work.

## The copy is made in the background

The copy exists as soon as you ask, and its setup is filled in over the next minute or so. Each
part is copied as a separate step, in order, with the scheduled tasks last, so a task never runs
on a copy that is still missing its plugins.

A step that fails does not undo the others. The copy is kept, and its report says which step
failed. If you do not want a half-made copy, delete it.

Over the API, the call answers with the copy's ID:

```bash
curl -X POST "$HOS_API/offices/off_9c1e7d/duplicate?workspaceId=ws_a1b2c3" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $HOS_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "name": "Front desk (West)" }'
```

```json
{
  "success": true,
  "officeId": "off_4d2a8b",
  "name": "Front desk (West)",
  "message": "The copy exists and is being filled in. Progress is in its duplication report."
}
```

Then read the copy's report until `status` is no longer `running`:

```bash
curl "$HOS_API/offices/off_4d2a8b/duplication?workspaceId=ws_a1b2c3" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $HOS_KEY"
```

`status` ends as `done`, `partial` (some steps failed) or `failed`. Each step lists what it copied
and anything it wants you to know, such as a scheduled task that could not be copied. See
[Get a duplication report](/docs/api-reference/offices/offices/getOfficeDuplication).

## Reconnect what the copy needs

The report's `notCopied` list names what the original had and the copy does not: its Telegram
or WhatsApp, its phone numbers and email addresses, the channels it was in, its connected
accounts, calendars and browsers. Each of these points at one office, or was granted to the
office by someone else, so giving it to the copy is a choice you make on the copy, not a side
effect of copying.

Give the copy only what its job needs. A copy for a new region usually needs its own phone
number, not the original's.
