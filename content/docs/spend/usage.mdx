---
title: Usage records
description: Every piece of model work, what it cost, who paid for it, and who spent it.
---

Every piece of model work leaves a usage record: which model ran, how many
tokens it used, what it cost, who paid, and who did the work. Records are written
for work on our model account **and** for work on your own model account, so one
list shows all model use whoever paid for it.

Usage records cover model use only. The cost of an office being awake is
reported separately; see [Compute](/docs/spend#compute).

## What one record says

| | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Model | The model that did the work |
| Tokens | Input, output, and the two kinds of cached tokens, counted separately |
| Who paid | Our model account (charged to your credit) or your own model account |
| Charged | What came out of your credit. **Zero for work on your own model account** |
| Cost | What the tokens cost, whoever paid. For work on your own model account, this is what your provider charges you |
| Who spent it | Nova, an AI employee, or a cloud browser, with the workspace and the person it ran for |
| When | The time the work was recorded |

The difference between **charged** and **cost** follows from
[Who pays for model use](/docs/spend#who-pays-for-model-use). For work
on our account they are equal. For work on your own account, charged is zero and
cost is the money you will see on your provider's bill. Budgets count cost, which
is why a budget can limit work we never charge for.

## Who spent it

Each record says what kind of work it was and where it ran.

| Kind | Means | Also names |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nova | Nova, in a chat, a scheduled task, or a text message conversation | The workspace, and the employee's office when Nova was working inside one |
| AI employee | An employee working in its office | The workspace and the employee |
| Cloud browser | A cloud browser session | The workspace and the browser |

Names are looked up when you read the records, not stored with them. A renamed
employee shows its current name on old records, and something that has since
been deleted is reported as missing, with its ID in place of a name.

Nova working at account level, rather than inside a workspace, shows as the
workspace **Console**.

A record also carries the person the work ran for, when there was one. A
scheduled task runs for the person who created it, so its spend is recorded
against them.

## Reading records

[List model usage](/docs/api-reference/billing/usage/listUsage) returns
records newest first, a page at a time:

#### Request

```bash
curl "$HOS_API/billing/usage?limit=2" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $HOS_KEY"
```

#### Response `200 OK`

```json
{
  "success": true,
  "entries": [
    {
      "id": "u_8sK2pQ0aLm",
      "modelId": "claude-sonnet-4-6",
      "inputTokens": 18234,
      "outputTokens": 912,
      "cacheWriteTokens": 0,
      "cacheReadTokens": 15200,
      "billingMode": "byok",
      "totalCostUsd": 0,
      "computedCostUsd": 0.0731,
      "uid": "usr_7f2a91",
      "workspaceId": "ws_a1b2c3",
      "officeId": "off_9c1e7d",
      "browserId": null,
      "origin": {
        "kind": "office",
        "workspace": { "id": "ws_a1b2c3", "name": "Support" },
        "office": { "id": "off_9c1e7d", "name": "Ada", "serviceId": "svc_41b0", "projectId": "prj_2c9e" },
        "browser": null,
        "missing": []
      },
      "createdAt": "2026-10-14T15:58:40.000Z"
    }

```

This entry ran on the customer's own model account: nothing was charged, and it
cost them about seven cents with their provider. To get the next page, send the
last entry's `id` as `startAfter`.

`office.serviceId` is the machine the employee runs on. It matches `serviceId`
in [Get compute spend](/docs/api-reference/billing/compute/getComputeSpendSummary),
which is how to put an employee's model use next to its machine time.

## Totals and charts

Two calls add records up for you. Both split every figure into what was charged
to your credit (`platformUsd`) and what work on your own model account cost
(`byokUsd`).

| Call | Gives |
| --- | --- |
| [Get a usage summary](/docs/api-reference/billing/usage/getUsageSummary) | Totals for each of the last few months, the month so far, and a total for every day |
| [Get a usage series](/docs/api-reference/billing/usage/getUsageSeries) | Totals for any window you choose, by hour, day, week or month |

Both take a `timezone`, so days and months line up with the calendar of whoever
is reading them. Without one they use UTC.

All of these need `billing:read` at `console`, so they show the whole account.
For one workspace's spend without account access,
[Get current spend](/docs/api-reference/workspaces/budgets/getWorkspaceSpend)
needs only `workspace:read` on that workspace, and returns its spend for the
current day, week or month. Each budget also reports its own spend so far; see
[Budgets](/docs/spend/budgets).
