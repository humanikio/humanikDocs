---
title: Budgets
description: Limits on model spend for the whole account, one workspace, or one employee, and what happens when one is reached.
---

A budget is a dollar limit on model use over a day, a week or a month. You
choose what it covers, what it counts, and what happens when spend reaches the
limit: stop the work, warn and keep going, or only tell people.

It is what makes unattended work safe to allow. You set the limit once, and your
employees work inside it without anyone approving each step.

<Callout>
  **Budgets cover model use only.** The cost of an office being awake is not
  counted and cannot be limited by a budget. See
  [What costs money](/docs/spend#what-costs-money).
</Callout>

## The parts of a budget

| Part | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Covers** | The whole account, one workspace, or one employee |
| **Resets** | Every day, every week, or every month |
| **Counts** | Spend charged to your credit, spend on your own model account, or both |
| **Limit** | An amount in US dollars |
| **At the limit** | Stop the work, warn and keep going, or only tell people |
| **Alerts** | Who is told, at what share of the limit, and whether by text message |

A budget can also be switched off, which keeps its settings, or paused until its
window resets.

## What a budget covers

| Covers | Counts spend from | Can stop work in | Who can manage it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The whole account** | Every workspace | Every workspace | `billing:manage` @ `console` |
| **One workspace** | That workspace, including all of its employees | That workspace | `workspace:manage` on the workspace, or `billing:manage` |
| **One employee** | That employee, plus Nova while it works inside that employee's office | That employee, and Nova working inside its office | `workspace:manage` on the workspace, or `billing:manage` |

What a budget covers is fixed when it is created. To move a limit from a
workspace to one employee, delete the budget and create another.

Because a workspace or employee budget needs only `workspace:manage`, you can
let a team lead set their own team's limits without showing them the account's
balance, usage or invoices. Account budgets need `billing:manage`.

<Callout type="warn">
  **A budget does not stop a cloud browser.** Browser spend is counted by
  account and workspace budgets, so it moves them toward their limits, but
  browser sessions are not checked against them and keep running.
</Callout>

## When the total resets

| Window | Runs from | To |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily | Midnight | The next midnight |
| Weekly | Monday at midnight | The next Monday at midnight |
| Monthly | Midnight on the 1st | Midnight on the next 1st |

**Whose midnight depends on what the budget covers.** Workspace and employee
budgets use the workspace's time zone, set with
[Set the timezone](/docs/api-reference/workspaces/workspaces/updateWorkspaceTimeConfig).
A workspace that has never had one set uses UTC. Two workspaces in different
regions therefore reset at different moments, which is what you want when they
serve customers in different places.

**Account budgets always use UTC**, because an account has no time zone of its
own and picking one workspace's would be arbitrary. Every budget reports its
reset time as `resetsAt`, so the moment is always visible.

Changes to daylight saving time are handled: a day is always a calendar day in
the workspace's zone, even when that day is 23 or 25 hours long.

## What a budget counts

| Counts | Adds up |
| --- | --- |
| Credit | Model use on our model account, charged to your credit |
| Your own model account | Model use on [your own model account](/docs/models/your-own-key), at what it cost you there. We never bill this |
| Both | The two added together |

**A budget only acts on work it counts.** A budget that counts only credit never
stops or warns about work on your own model account, and a budget that counts
only your own account never stops work on credit. Count both to limit model use
whoever is paying for it.

Limiting your own model account is the part most people do not expect. We are
not paid for that usage, and a budget limits it anyway, so an employee that keeps
spending on your own key stops at your limit rather than at your provider's.

## What happens at the limit

A budget has reached its limit when the spend it counts in the current window
is equal to or more than the limit.

| | **Stop the work** | **Warn and keep going** | **Only tell people** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| In the API | `block` | `warn` | `notify` |
| New work | Refused | Runs | Runs |
| Work already running | Stopped at its next step | Continues | Continues |
| Shown in the app | The refusal and its reason | The run is marked as over the limit | Nothing |
| Alerts with none configured | None | One, at the limit | One, at the limit |
| Recorded as refused work | Yes | No | No |

### Stop the work

New work by Nova or an AI employee is refused until the window resets, the limit
is raised, or the budget is paused. Work already running stops at its next step:
Nova checks between steps of a turn, and an employee checks before each call to
a model.

What the caller sees:

| Where | Refusal |
| --- | --- |
| Nova, before a turn starts | `402` with `code: "budget_exceeded"`, the budget's ID, window and scope, the amount used and the limit |
| Nova, partway through a turn | `200` with `status: "budget_exceeded"`, and the turn ends |
| An AI employee asked to start work | `402` with `code: "budget_exceeded"` |

The message reads like *Weekly workspace budget reached ("Support weekly").
Resets on Monday at midnight (workspace timezone).* Branch on the code, not the
words.

<Callout type="warn">
  **Spend can pass the limit.** Spend is recorded when a piece of work finishes,
  and a check result is reused for up to five seconds. So the work that carries
  the total over the limit finishes its current step, and other work that
  started at the same moment may too. Set the limit with some room.
</Callout>

If the spend for a budget that could stop the work cannot be read at that
moment, the work is stopped rather than allowed. The refusal may then name no
budget.

### Warn and keep going

The work runs. In the app, a run that started while the budget was over its
limit is marked as over the limit, with the budget's name and the amount used,
so the person reading it knows. This mark is shown in the app's chat and is not
returned by the API.

For an AI employee the mark is set when a run starts. If the budget goes over
its limit during a long run, the mark appears on the next one.

### Only tell people

The work runs and nothing in it changes. The budget exists only to send
[alerts](#alerts).

The rules, in the order they are applied:

<Steps>
<Step>
### Find the budgets that apply

The account's budgets, the budgets on the workspace the work runs in, and the
budgets on the employee doing it. Budgets on other workspaces and other
employees are ignored.
</Step>

<Step>
### Skip what is off

A switched off budget, or one paused until its reset, is skipped entirely. It
stops nothing, warns about nothing and sends no alerts.
</Step>

<Step>
### Keep the ones that count this work

Only a budget that counts this kind of spend can stop it or warn about it. Work
on your own model account is not stopped by a budget that counts only credit.
</Step>

<Step>
### Any stopping budget at its limit stops the work

If one or more budgets set to stop the work are at their limit, the work stops,
whatever any other budget says. The refusal names the one with the **lowest
limit** among them.
</Step>

<Step>
### Otherwise the work runs

If a budget set to warn is at its limit, the run is marked as over the limit. A
budget set to only tell people never changes the work.
</Step>
</Steps>

The practical result: **the tightest budget that stops work wins.** Adding a
budget can only make a stop more likely, never less. A generous account budget
does not loosen a strict workspace one.

## Alerts

Alerts tell people as spend approaches and reaches a limit. They work the same
for all three actions.

**Thresholds are shares of the limit.** `0.5` is 50 percent, `1` is the limit
itself, and values up to `2` (twice the limit) are allowed, so a budget that
only warns can alert again well past its limit. Up to ten thresholds.

**With no thresholds set**, a budget that warns or only tells people still
alerts once, at the limit. A budget that stops the work sends nothing, because
the refusal is the signal. Add thresholds to a stopping budget to hear about it
before work stops.

**Each threshold is sent once per window.** A new day, week or month starts with
nothing sent. If several thresholds are crossed at once, one alert is sent, for
the highest. Changing the limit, the window, what is counted, or the alerts lets
every threshold be sent again in the current window.

### Who is told

| | Recipients |
| --- | --- |
| You list people | Exactly those people, up to 50 |
| You list nobody | Everyone holding `billing:manage` on the account, plus, for a workspace or employee budget, that workspace's admins |

Alerts arrive as notifications in the app. For a workspace or employee budget
they appear in that workspace. For an account budget they appear in the
workspace whose work crossed the threshold, since there is no account level
notification list.

### Text messages

Switch on text messages for a budget and recipients also get a text from the
platform number at the thresholds you choose. **By default that is only at the
limit.** A text goes only to people who have confirmed a phone number on their
profile; anyone else still gets the notification in the app.

### When an alert is sent

Alerts are checked when Nova or an AI employee is about to start work, and
between the steps of a Nova turn. They are not sent on a timer. Spend is
recorded as work finishes, so the alert for a threshold arrives when the next
piece of work in that scope starts. If nothing else runs, the alert waits until
something does.

## Pausing until the reset

Pausing sets a budget aside until its current window ends. Until then it stops
nothing, warns about nothing and sends no alerts. At the reset it applies again
on its own, and nothing has to be undone. You can also clear a pause early.

This is the way to let work continue past a limit for the rest of a day, week or
month, without raising the limit and having to remember to lower it again.
Pausing needs `billing:manage`.

| To | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Let work continue until the window resets | Pause |
| Stop applying a budget until you turn it back on | Switch it off |
| Allow more spend from now on | Raise the limit |
| Remove it and its record of refused work | Delete it |

## The record of refused work

Each time a budget set to stop the work refuses something, a record is kept: when
it happened, what kind of work it was (for example Nova in a chat, a scheduled
Nova task, or an AI employee), which workspace and employee it was in, and the
person it ran for.

Read it with
[List refused work](/docs/api-reference/billing/budgets/listBudgetEvents),
newest first. It answers *"what has this limit actually stopped?"*, which is the
question to ask before raising one.

The record is kept on a best effort basis. A refusal never fails because it
could not be recorded, so the list can occasionally miss one. Deleting a budget
deletes its record.

## Examples

All of these use the account-wide budget routes, which need `billing:manage`.
Workspace and employee budgets can also be managed with only `workspace:manage`
through the [workspace routes](/docs/api-reference/workspaces/budgets/listWorkspaceBudgets).

### Cap one employee at $50 a month, and get a text at the limit

Find the employee's office ID first, with
[List offices](/docs/api-reference/offices/offices/listOffices):

```bash
curl "$HOS_API/offices?workspaceId=ws_a1b2c3" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $HOS_KEY"
```

Then create the budget:

#### Request

```bash
curl -X POST "$HOS_API/billing/budgets" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $HOS_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "name": "Ada monthly",
    "scope": { "kind": "office", "workspaceId": "ws_a1b2c3", "officeId": "off_9c1e7d" },
    "window": "monthly",
    "metric": ["platform", "byok"],
    "limitUsd": 50,
    "action": "block",
    "alerts": { "thresholds": [0.8, 1], "recipients": [], "sms": true }
  }'
```

#### Response `201 Created`

```json
{
  "success": true,
  "budget": {
    "id": "b7Rk2Wq9LmXv4Pz0Ns1d",
    "name": "Ada monthly",
    "scope": { "kind": "office", "workspaceId": "ws_a1b2c3", "officeId": "off_9c1e7d" },
    "workspaceId": "ws_a1b2c3",
    "window": "monthly",
    "metric": ["platform", "byok"],
    "limitUsd": 50,
    "action": "block",
    "alerts": { "thresholds": [0.8, 1], "recipients": [], "sms": true },
    "enabled": true,
    "pausedUntil": null,
    "pausedBy": null,
    "usage": {
      "usedUsd": 0,
      "windowStart": "2026-10-01T04:00:00.000Z",
      "resetsAt": "2026-11-01T04:00:00.000Z"
    },
    "workspaceName": "Support",
    "officeName": "Ada"
  }
}
```

What happens: the month runs on the Support workspace's calendar. At $40 the
billing admins and Support's workspace admins get a notification. At $50 they
get another, and those with a confirmed phone also get a text, because `smsAt`
was left out and defaults to the limit. From then until the 1st, Ada refuses new
work and stops running work at its next step. The rest of the workspace is not
affected.

### A monthly account limit that only warns

#### Request

```bash
curl -X POST "$HOS_API/billing/budgets" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $HOS_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "name": "Account monthly",
    "scope": { "kind": "account" },
    "window": "monthly",
    "metric": ["platform", "byok"],
    "limitUsd": 2000,
    "action": "warn",
    "alerts": { "thresholds": [0.5, 0.9, 1], "recipients": [], "sms": false }
  }'
```

What happens: the month runs from midnight UTC on the 1st. Billing admins are
told at $1,000, $1,800 and $2,000. Past $2,000 nothing stops: new Nova and
employee runs in every workspace are marked as over the limit in the app until
the month resets. To
make the account limit firm later, change `action` to `block` with
[Update a budget](/docs/api-reference/billing/budgets/updateBudget).

### Let a team lead manage their own limit

A person or service account holding `workspaceAdmin` on one workspace, and
nothing on the console, can create a budget for it through the workspace route. They never see the
account's balance or invoices.

#### Request

```bash
curl -X POST "$HOS_API/workspaces/ws_a1b2c3/budgets" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $HOS_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "name": "Support weekly",
    "window": "weekly",
    "metric": ["platform"],
    "limitUsd": 300,
    "action": "block",
    "alerts": { "thresholds": [0.75], "recipients": [], "sms": false }
  }'
```

`scope` is left out, so this is a workspace budget. The week starts on Monday at
midnight in the workspace's time zone. It counts only credit, so employees in
Support that run on your own model account are not stopped by it.

### Let work continue for the rest of the month

A budget has stopped work and you would rather not raise the limit. Pause it
until its window resets. A workspace or employee budget needs its `workspaceId`
on the call; an account budget does not.

#### Request

```bash
curl -X POST "$HOS_API/billing/budgets/b7Rk2Wq9LmXv4Pz0Ns1d/pause?workspaceId=ws_a1b2c3" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $HOS_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "untilReset": true }'
```

The response returns the budget with `pausedUntil` set to the start of next
month. Send `{ "untilReset": false }` to clear the pause early.

## What a budget does not do

- **It does not count compute.** An office being awake costs money that no
  budget sees.
- **It does not stop cloud browsers**, though their spend is counted.
- **It does not cover phone calls or text messages.** Nothing meters them.
- **It does not stop work mid-step.** The step that crosses the limit finishes,
  so spend can end up above the limit.
- **It does not reset on your local time for an account budget.** Account
  budgets use UTC.
