---
title: Credit and top ups
description: The prepaid balance that model use and machine time are paid from, what draws it down, and how it is refilled.
---

Model work that runs on our model account, and the machine time your services
use on Humanik Cloud, are paid from one prepaid balance held for your account, in
US dollars. This page covers what draws on it, what happens when it runs low, and
how to keep it filled. Model work on
[your own model account](/docs/models/your-own-key) never touches it.

## What draws it down

| Spend | Drawn from your credit |
| --- | --- |
| Nova, an AI employee or a cloud browser, on our model account | **Yes**, at what the work cost |
| The same work on your own model account | **No**. Your provider bills you, and we charge nothing |
| Machines running (offices, browsers, web services) | **Yes**, once an hour, at the [price of each machine](/docs/cloud/pricing) |

Each piece of model work is charged once, when it finishes, at what its tokens
cost. The charge is recorded in your [usage records](/docs/spend/usage), and the
same figure is what [budgets](/docs/spend/budgets) count.

## What model work is charged at

Model work on our account runs through OpenRouter, and **you are charged what OpenRouter
charged for that request**. Nova and AI employees are charged the same way. OpenRouter
reports the charge for each request, so the figure already includes what the model's
vendor charges for cached tokens, longer prompts and any other pricing rule. We do not
work it out again from a price list of our own.

- **One piece of work, many requests.** A task an AI employee works on can make many model
  requests, for example one for each tool it uses. Its charge is the total of all of them.
- **When OpenRouter does not report a charge** for a request, that request is
  priced from the model's published price per token instead.
- **On your own OpenRouter account**, nothing is charged to your credit, and the cost in your
  usage records is what OpenRouter charged your account.

Machine time is charged once an hour, as described below. Budgets do not count
it.

A new account starts with a balance of zero. Some accounts receive a one time
trial credit when they are set up; it appears on your invoices as a trial
credit with nothing charged.

## The hourly charge for machine time

About 2 minutes after each hour ends, the machine time your account used in that
hour is taken from the balance as one charge, with one line for each service
that ran. Time is measured exactly for each machine, with no minimum and no
rounding. Because a charge is made only after its hour ends, the newest hour can
be up to about an hour behind on the charged figures.

The charge is always taken in full, so it can take the balance below zero. Then:

1. If the balance is below your threshold, an
   [automatic top up](#automatic-top-ups) runs, if you have them on.
2. If the balance is still below zero after that, **every running machine on the
   account is stopped**, and new machine starts are refused until the balance is
   $1 or more.

A request to a sleeping service while starts are paused is refused with a `402`
and this message:

```
Machine starts are paused for this account: its credit balance went below zero. Machines start again once the balance is $1 or more. Add credit and the service starts on the next request.
```

Adding credit is enough. The next start checks the balance and goes ahead.

Each hourly charge is recorded with the balance before and after it, what the
top up did, and whether the account was left below zero. Read them with
[List compute bills](/docs/api-reference/billing/compute/listComputeBills), and
the per service lines with
[List compute charges](/docs/api-reference/billing/compute/listComputeCharges).

## When the balance runs low

Before work on our model account starts, and again while it runs, the balance is
checked.

**Work is refused when the balance is at or below five cents** and automatic top
ups are off. Nova answers with a `402` and `code: "insufficient_balance"`, and an
AI employee is refused in the same way.

**The work that crosses that floor is allowed to finish.** The check happens
between steps, not in the middle of one, so the step that takes the balance
below five cents completes, and the balance can end below zero. The next
check refuses.

**The account owner is told.** When work is refused this way, the owner is sent
an email, at most once a day. In the app, only the owner is shown the prompt to
add credit or a card. Everyone else is told to contact their administrator.

Work on your own model account is never refused for low credit, because it does
not use any.

**Machine time draws on the same balance.** An hourly charge for machine time
can take the balance to five cents or below, and model work on our model account
is then refused in the same way.

To find out in advance whether work would be allowed, call
[Check before spending](/docs/api-reference/billing/credit/checkBalance). It
answers without doing any work, which is useful before starting something long.

## Adding credit

Credit is bought in the console with a saved card, between $5 and $500 at a
time. A platform fee is added on top of the amount, and the console shows the
amount, the fee and the total before the card is charged. The credit lands in
your balance once the payment succeeds.

Buying credit, saving cards and choosing the default card are console flows. An
API key cannot do them.

## Automatic top ups

An automatic top up refills the balance from your default card when it falls
below a threshold you choose.

| Setting | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Threshold | Top up once the balance falls below this. Must be more than zero |
| Amount | How much credit to add each time. At least $5 |

When a piece of model work or an hourly charge for machine time leaves the
balance below the threshold, the default card is charged for the amount plus the
platform fee, the credit is added, and an invoice is written. The top up happens
**after** the charge that took the balance below the threshold, not before it.

Switch it on, change it or switch it off with
[Configure automatic top ups](/docs/api-reference/billing/credit/updateAutoReload):

#### Request

```bash
curl -X POST "$HOS_API/billing/balance/auto-reload" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $HOS_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "autoReload": { "enabled": true, "thresholdUsd": 10, "reloadAmountUsd": 50 } }'
```

#### Response `200 OK`

```json
{ "success": true }
```

Send `"autoReload": null` to switch it off. This needs `billing:manage`.

<Callout type="warn">
  **A failed top up does not tell you.** If the card is declined, or there is no
  default card, no credit is added and no notification or email is sent. The out
  of credit email is sent only when automatic top ups are off, so it does not
  cover this case either. The charge is
  tried again after later usage. If your balance is lower than you expect, check
  the default card in the console first. For top ups after an hourly charge for
  machine time, [List compute bills](/docs/api-reference/billing/compute/listComputeBills)
  shows what each attempt did.
</Callout>

## Invoices

Credit invoices record money coming into your balance. Each one carries the
credit added, the platform fee, the total charged to the card, and a status.

| Type | Recorded when |
| --- | --- |
| Credit purchase | You bought credit in the console |
| Automatic top up | A top up charged your card |
| Trial credit | A trial credit was granted. Nothing was charged |

List them with
[List credit invoices](/docs/api-reference/billing/usage/listInvoices).

**Subscription invoices**, for platform access, are not in that list. They are
viewed and paid in the console.
