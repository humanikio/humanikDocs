---
title: Actions
description: Every permission the platform checks, and what each one allows.
---

Every gated operation checks one action at one resource. This is the full
list.

You need it when writing a custom role. For built-in roles, which cover most
cases, see [Roles and policies](/docs/access-control/roles).

<Callout>
  **An action alone is not access.** A grant is an action *and* a resource, and
  both have to match. `office:manage` assigned at one workspace grants nothing
  at another. See [Granting access](/docs/access-control/assignments).
</Callout>

The **Usually written at** column is the resource level a grant normally names.
A parent covers its descendants, so an action listed at `office` can also be
granted at the workspace above it to cover every office in it.
